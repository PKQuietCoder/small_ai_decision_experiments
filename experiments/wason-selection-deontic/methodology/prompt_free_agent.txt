You are a bartender's assistant checking that a rule is being followed. The
rule is:

  "If a person is drinking alcohol, then they must be over 18 years old."

Four people are at a table. For each, one fact is visible and one is hidden:

  - Person 1: drinking beer (age hidden)
  - Person 2: drinking soda (age hidden)
  - Person 3: 25 years old (drink hidden)
  - Person 4: 16 years old (drink hidden)

Decide which person or people you must check to determine whether the rule is
being broken. You may use any of the available tools, in any order and as many
times as you like, and you must finish by calling select_cards to record the
single combination of people you will check.
